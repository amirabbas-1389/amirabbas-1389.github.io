2026 U.S. Congressional Midterm Elections: What to Watch

A neutral, source-first overview of the November 3, 2026 elections for the U.S. House and Senate.

Election date and seats

The 2026 U.S. congressional midterm elections are scheduled for November 3, 2026. Every House seat and roughly one-third of Senate seats are up for election. All 435 House seats are elected every two years, while Senate elections are staggered. USA.gov

Missouri redistricting dispute

On September 21, Reuters reported that the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit backed a Republican-drawn congressional map in Missouri. The ruling was paused until September 28 to give the U.S. Supreme Court time to respond; Missouri officials were using the prior map while the dispute continued.

Polling context

A Reuters/Ipsos poll conducted in September among 1,277 U.S. adults reported a 32% approval rating for President Donald Trump. Among registered voters asked which party they would support for Congress, the reported responses were 43% Democratic and 35% Republican. The poll reported a 3-percentage-point margin of error. This is a dated national poll, not a forecast of individual congressional races.

How ORYNT will cover the election

ORYNT will identify the date and source for election updates, distinguish campaign statements from independently reported facts, and keep polling results tied to their stated population and methodology.
