U.S. Presidents, Vice Presidents & Inaugurations

A visual reference to the presidents and vice presidents of the United States, their periods of service, and the history of presidential inaugurations from 1789 to the present.

Historical note: the United States did not have photography during its earliest presidencies. For those periods, surviving visual records include paintings, engravings, prints and other historical documents rather than photographs.
