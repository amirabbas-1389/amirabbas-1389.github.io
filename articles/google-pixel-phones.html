Google Pixel Phones: The Pixel 10 Generation

Google's tenth-generation Pixel lineup combines a refreshed hardware design with Tensor G5, Gemini Nano, upgraded cameras and new AI features.

Google's Pixel 10 generation was introduced in August 2025 and includes the Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10 Pro XL and Pixel 10 Pro Fold. Google positioned the lineup around its Tensor G5 chip, Gemini Nano and a redesigned hardware platform with improved cameras, displays and charging.

Pixel 10, Pro and Pro XL

The standard Pixel 10 has a 6.3-inch Actua display and introduces a 5x telephoto camera to the regular Pixel tier. Google lists the Pixel 10 with 256GB storage at a starting price of $799 at launch. The Pixel 10 Pro has a 6.3-inch display, while the Pro XL expands to 6.8 inches. Both Pro models use a triple rear-camera system and support Pro Res Zoom up to 100x.

Tensor G5: the chip behind the new Pixels

Tensor G5 is Google's custom silicon for the Pixel 10 generation. Google describes it as the company's most significant Tensor upgrade since the original Tensor and says it was co-designed with Google DeepMind to run the latest Gemini Nano model on-device.

AI features

Pixel 10 introduces features including Magic Cue and Camera Coach. Magic Cue can proactively surface useful information in supported apps, while Camera Coach uses Gemini models to suggest photography improvements. Google also uses Tensor G5 and its imaging models for Pro Res Zoom on the Pro phones.

A new Pixel design

The Pixel 10 series keeps Google's distinctive camera bar while refining the overall design. Google says the phones use more recycled materials than previous Pixel generations and add built-in Qi2 wireless charging through Pixelsnap-compatible hardware. The standard Pixel 10 is available in Obsidian, Frost, Indigo and Lemongrass, while the Pro models add finishes including Moonstone and Jade.

Pixel 10 Pro Fold

The Pixel 10 Pro Fold takes the Pixel platform into a larger foldable form. It has an 8-inch inner display and a 6.4-inch outer display, a gearless hinge, IP68 water and dust resistance, and Tensor G5. Google also says the foldable has a 48MP main camera within a triple rear-camera system and supports Super Res Zoom up to 20x.

Why the Pixel 10 generation matters

Google's approach is notable because the company is combining its own hardware, custom silicon, Android software and Gemini AI rather than treating the phone as only a hardware product. The Pixel 10 generation therefore represents Google's current attempt to make on-device AI a central part of the smartphone experience.

Quick takeaway

  • Pixel 10: the regular model with a 6.3-inch display and a new 5x telephoto camera.
  • Pixel 10 Pro: the 6.3-inch premium model with a triple camera and up to 100x Pro Res Zoom.
  • Pixel 10 Pro XL: the larger 6.8-inch Pro model with the same Pro-focused camera platform.
  • Pixel 10 Pro Fold: Google's foldable Pixel with an 8-inch inner display and Tensor G5.
  • Tensor G5: the custom Google chip supporting the generation's on-device AI features.

Specifications and launch pricing in this article are based on Google's published information for the Pixel 10 generation. Availability and pricing can differ by country and may change over time.
