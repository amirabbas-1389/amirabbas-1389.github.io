About ORYNT

ORYNT is an independent publishing project covering science, space, technology, computing, research and major world affairs. Its editorial approach emphasizes clear explanations, context and traceable sources.

Audience and metrics

ORYNT is being prepared for an international, English-language audience, with a Persian-language expansion planned as a complementary path. We will publish verified traffic and newsletter metrics here as they become available rather than presenting estimates as facts.

Brand-safety and editorial separation

Sponsored placements will be visibly identified. Sponsors will not receive editorial control over independent ORYNT reporting. We also avoid deceptive functionality, hidden commercial links and practices that could undermine search or reader trust.
