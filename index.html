BOOK OF THE MONTH

The Midnight Library

Matt Haig · Fiction

A story about choices, regret and the lives we imagine.

The Midnight Library follows Nora Seed as she encounters a mysterious library containing possible versions of her life. Each book represents a different path she might have taken, turning the novel into a reflection on decisions, missed opportunities and what gives a life meaning.

At its deeper level, the novel explores regret, possibility, identity and the gap between an imagined perfect life and an actual human life. Rather than treating every choice as a simple success-or-failure calculation, it asks how our understanding of a meaningful life can change when we see its complexity.
