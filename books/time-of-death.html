ABOUT THE STORY

A personal search set against a changing nation

TIME OF DEATH follows Robert F. Nelson across decades of personal and public change. After his son Michael disappears during the Korean War, Nelson’s search becomes a defining force in his life. As his career carries him through military and political power, the story moves through the Vietnam era and Watergate, tracing the tension between private loss and public duty.

At its heart, the novel explores how a family’s unanswered questions can echo through a lifetime—and how history shapes the people asked to serve it.
