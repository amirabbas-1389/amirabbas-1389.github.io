The History of Apple AirPods

From the original AirPods in 2016 to the AirPods 5 and AirPods Max 2 announced in 2026, Apple's wireless-audio line has expanded across open-ear, in-ear and over-ear designs.

2016: The original AirPods

Apple introduced the first AirPods on September 7, 2016, alongside the iPhone 7. They were designed as completely wireless earbuds and used Apple's W1 chip for pairing, power management and communication with Apple devices. Apple said the original AirPods offered up to five hours of listening on one charge, with more than 24 hours available through the charging case. citeturn0search0

2019: AirPods 2 and AirPods Pro

AirPods 2 arrived in 2019, while Apple introduced the first AirPods Pro later that year. The Pro line added a different in-ear design and active noise cancellation, creating a separate premium branch of the AirPods family. Apple's archive records both launches in 2019. citeturn0search3

In September 2024, Apple introduced AirPods 4 in two versions: standard AirPods 4 and AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation. Apple also announced hearing-health features for AirPods Pro 2, including Hearing Protection, a Hearing Test and a clinical-grade Hearing Aid feature in supported regions. Why AirPods became a major Apple product line

AirPods were designed around a close connection between hardware, software and Apple's device ecosystem. Over time, Apple expanded the family rather than replacing the original concept with a single design: open-ear AirPods, AirPods Pro and the over-ear AirPods Max now serve different listening formats and feature sets.
