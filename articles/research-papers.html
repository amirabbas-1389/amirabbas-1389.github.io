What makes a research paper useful?

A research paper is more than a conclusion: its methods, evidence, limitations and context determine how useful its findings are.

Read the method

Look at how the researchers gathered data, what controls or comparisons were used and what assumptions shape the analysis.

Read the limitations

Good research communicates uncertainty and boundaries. A result can be meaningful without proving a much broader claim.
