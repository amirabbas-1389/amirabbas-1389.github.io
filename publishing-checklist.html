ORYNT Publishing Checklist

  • Confirm important claims against a primary source or credible research paper.
  • Separate NEWS, ANALYSIS and EXPLAINER labels.
  • Attribute company claims, forecasts and future plans.
  • Check publication and update dates.
  • Add title, description and canonical URL.
  • Add the article to its category and Latest page.
  • Update sitemap for new indexable pages.
  • Review links and mobile layout before publication.
