Princess Diana

What did the major investigations find about the claims of a deliberate plot?

The claim

Was Diana deliberately killed?

Claims have alleged that the crash in Paris was part of a planned assassination. ORYNT separates those claims from the findings of official investigations.

What the investigations show

Operation Paget examined the conspiracy allegation

Operation Paget was established to investigate allegations of a conspiracy to murder Diana, Princess of Wales, and Dodi Al Fayed. The UK government also published records concerning the death and related investigation. citeturn0search4

The evidence-first approach is important here: the existence of an investigation into a claim is not evidence that the claim itself was established.

Important uncertainty

Separate claims need separate evidence

Claims about intelligence agencies, the Royal Family, vehicle interference or other alleged actors should not be bundled together. Each would require its own specific evidence. A historical allegation should not be treated as established merely because it has been repeated for years.

How to evaluate the case

QuestionEvidence-first approach
Was there an investigation into conspiracy allegations?Yes. Operation Paget specifically examined allegations of a conspiracy.
Does the existence of Operation Paget prove a conspiracy?No. An investigation tests an allegation; it does not establish the allegation by itself.
How should new claims be treated?Check whether they are supported by primary records, authenticated evidence and independent corroboration.

Bottom line

The responsible way to discuss Diana's death is to distinguish documented facts, investigative findings, witness accounts and later allegations. ORYNT will update this article if authoritative records materially change the documented picture.
