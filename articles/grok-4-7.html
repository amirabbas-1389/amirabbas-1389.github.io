Grok 4.7: xAI's New AI Model Focuses on Coding and Complex Work

xAI introduced Grok 4.7 on September 21, 2026, with a larger base model, longer training on difficult tasks and a stronger emphasis on checking its own work.

xAI announced Grok 4.7 as its latest model for coding, agentic tasks and knowledge work. The company says the model is designed to spend longer on difficult tasks, manage longer context and verify its work more carefully. citeturn2view1

What's new?

xAI says Grok 4.7 uses a new, larger base model than Grok 4.6 and was trained with a longer reinforcement-learning run focused more heavily on tasks that can take hours to complete. It was also trained to understand the Grok Bot harness natively. citeturn2view1

Coding and knowledge work

The launch places a strong emphasis on software engineering and multi-step professional work. xAI published results for coding, electrical engineering, office work, legal work and clinical reasoning benchmarks. These are vendor-reported benchmark results, so they are best read as measurements under the specific tests and settings described by xAI rather than as a universal ranking of AI systems. citeturn2view1

Availability

Grok 4.7 is available through the xAI API and is also offered through Grok Build, Cursor and other model platforms. xAI also offers a faster variant through Grok Build and Cursor at higher token rates. citeturn2view1turn4search2

Why this release matters

The direction of the release is clear: rather than focusing only on short conversational answers, xAI is targeting longer-running coding and knowledge-work tasks where an AI system must plan, use context, check intermediate results and keep working toward a final output.
