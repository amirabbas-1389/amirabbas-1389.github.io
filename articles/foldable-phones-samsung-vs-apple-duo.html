Foldable Phones in 2026: Samsung Galaxy Z vs Apple iPhone Duo

Samsung has spent years developing foldable phones. In 2026, Apple's first foldable iPhone enters the same category. Here is how the current Galaxy Z Fold7, Galaxy Z Flip7 and iPhone Duo compare on hardware, displays, cameras, software, battery and price.

1. Two different ideas of a foldable phone

Samsung's two current Galaxy Z designs show that "foldable phone" is not one single category. The Galaxy Z Fold7 opens like a small tablet, giving users a large internal screen while keeping a conventional cover screen on the outside. The Galaxy Z Flip7 folds a conventional-sized smartphone in half, making the closed device much smaller.

3. Cameras

Samsung takes two different approaches. Galaxy Z Fold7 has a triple rear camera system: a 200MP wide camera, a 12MP ultrawide and a 10MP telephoto with 3x optical zoom. Galaxy Z Flip7 uses a simpler dual rear setup with a 50MP wide camera and 12MP ultrawide camera. citeturn0search1turn6search0

4. Performance and software

Galaxy Z Fold7 uses Qualcomm's Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy and runs Samsung's Android-based software with Galaxy AI features. Samsung offers configurations with 12GB or 16GB of memory and up to 1TB of storage. citeturn0search2

Galaxy Z Flip7 uses Samsung's Exynos 2500, with 12GB of RAM and 256GB or 512GB of storage. It runs Android 16 with One UI 8 and Samsung lists seven years of OS and security updates. citeturn6search12

iPhone Duo is powered by Apple's A20 Pro, the same chip used in the iPhone 18 Pro generation. 5. Battery and charging

Galaxy Z Fold7 has a 4,400mAh battery, while Galaxy Z Flip7 has a 4,300mAh battery. Samsung says the Fold7 can provide up to 24 hours of video playback and the Flip7 up to 31 hours under its stated test conditions. citeturn0search1turn6search0

Apple reports up to 31 hours of video playback using the inner display, up to 44 hours using the outer display, and up to 24 hours when both displays are used equally. Apple also says the phone can reach 50 percent charge in around 20 minutes with wired fast charging under its specified conditions. citeturn1view0

Galaxy Z Fold7 and Z Flip7 use Samsung's Armor Aluminum frame and FlexHinge designs and carry an IP48 rating. Fold7 uses Gorilla Glass Ceramic 2 on the cover and Gorilla Glass Victus 2 on the rear, while Flip7 uses Gorilla Glass Victus 2. citeturn0search1turn6search0

The bigger picture

The arrival of iPhone Duo changes the foldable-phone market by adding Apple's hardware and software ecosystem to a category where Samsung already has multiple form factors. The two companies are not simply making identical products: Samsung offers both book-style and clamshell designs, while Apple's first foldable iPhone is a book-style device.

For users comparing these phones, the most important differences are therefore not limited to processor specifications. Screen size and shape, the folded form factor, camera configuration, software ecosystem, multitasking features, durability and price all shape the experience.
