ABOUT ORYNT

Science · Technology · World.

ORYNT is an independent publishing project built to make important developments easier to understand through clear writing, context and traceable sources.

What we cover

ORYNT focuses on science, space, technology, computing, research and major world affairs. Coverage is designed to distinguish reported facts, analysis and explainers.

How we work

We prioritize primary sources, research papers, official statements and clearly attributed reporting. Forward-looking claims are identified as claims, and uncertainty is not presented as certainty.

Editorial goal

The goal is simple: help readers understand what is happening, why it matters and what evidence supports the story.
