Founding Sponsorship

We are opening a limited number of founding sponsorship opportunities for organizations whose work is relevant to ORYNT readers. Sponsorship can support a section, newsletter edition or clearly identified feature placement.

Three simple packages

PackageIncludesBest for
Section PartnerBrand acknowledgment + placement on one ORYNT section page for an agreed campaign periodTechnology, education and science companies
Feature PartnerClearly labeled sponsored placement around one selected feature or special projectProduct launches and major announcements
Newsletter PartnerClearly labeled sponsor placement in one newsletter editionBrands seeking direct reader attention

Final placement, duration and pricing are agreed individually. ORYNT does not sell editorial control: sponsored material is clearly identified and independent editorial decisions remain with ORYNT.

What we can offer

  • Clean, non-intrusive brand placement
  • Science and technology-focused context
  • Clear sponsor disclosure
  • Campaigns built around specific sections or launches
  • Custom packages for organizations with a strong editorial fit

What we do not offer

We do not present paid promotion as independent journalism, hide commercial relationships, or allow a sponsor to rewrite unrelated editorial coverage. This separation is part of the trust model we are building for ORYNT.
