Saudi Arabia's New Electric Cars: CEER Reveals EXOBOT

Saudi automotive brand CEER has unveiled its first flagship electric vehicles: an electric sedan and SUV under the EXOBOT name.

On September 21, 2026, Saudi Arabia's CEER revealed its first vehicles at a world premiere in Riyadh. The company presented an electric sedan and an SUV as its flagship EXOBOT models. PIF says the vehicles were designed, engineered and manufactured in Saudi Arabia. citeturn0search2

A new Saudi automotive brand

CEER was launched by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund in 2022 as the country's first Saudi vehicle brand. The company is being developed as part of a wider effort to build a local automotive manufacturing ecosystem. citeturn0search2

Two vehicles at the first reveal

The EXOBOT reveal included both a sedan and an SUV. According to PIF, the pair are part of a planned portfolio of seven models that will be introduced over the next five years, including midsize and compact vehicles with different propulsion options.

Where will the cars be made?

PIF says the vehicles will be manufactured at the CEER Manufacturing Complex (CMC), described by the fund as the largest automotive production facility in the Middle East. The EXOBOT designs are described as being inspired by Saudi Arabia's landscape and culture.

What comes next?

The September reveal is an important product milestone, but detailed retail specifications, final market pricing and delivery schedules for individual EXOBOT variants were not included in the PIF announcement. Those details should be treated as pending until CEER publishes them.
