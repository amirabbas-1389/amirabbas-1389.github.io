How it works

Country and currency information

The country list and currency codes are loaded from the REST Countries project. A country can use more than one currency, and several countries may share a currency.

Banknote photographs

Images are searched live through Wikimedia Commons. Each image links to its Commons file page so you can check the original source and licensing details.

Educational reference only. The image search may return historical, withdrawn or collectible notes as well as current banknotes; check the issuing central bank for current legal tender. Currency names and image availability depend on the external services being reachable.
