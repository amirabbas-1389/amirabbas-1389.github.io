Xiaomi Cars and Bluetooth Products: SU7, YU7, Earbuds and Speakers

Xiaomi’s “Human × Car × Home” strategy connects electric vehicles with personal electronics. Here is a look at its electric-car lineup and a selection of wireless audio products.

Xiaomi SU7: the electric sedan

The SU7 is Xiaomi’s first production electric sedan. Xiaomi introduced the model in 2024 and launched a new-generation SU7 in March 2026. The company lists Standard, Pro and Max versions, with different battery, range and performance configurations. Range figures published for China use the CLTC test cycle and should not be directly compared with EPA or WLTP ratings.

For the new-generation model, Xiaomi has announced CLTC ranges of up to 902 km for the Pro, 835 km for the Max and 720 km for the Standard version. Actual range varies with trim, weather, speed, road conditions and use of climate control.

Xiaomi YU7: an electric SUV

The YU7 is Xiaomi’s first SUV, launched in 2025. Xiaomi describes it as a mid-to-large electric SUV, with a spacious cabin, advanced driver-assistance hardware and an integrated digital cockpit. The company lists CLTC range figures of up to 835 km for the YU7, depending on configuration.

The YU7 and SU7 are currently sold in mainland China. Xiaomi Auto’s global website says the brand is preparing to come to Europe in 2027; a global website or promotional gallery does not mean every displayed model is already available in every country.

Bluetooth earbuds and open-ear audio

Xiaomi’s wireless audio range includes in-ear true-wireless earbuds and open-ear designs. Examples listed on Xiaomi’s global product catalog include Xiaomi Buds 5 Pro, REDMI Buds 6 and Xiaomi OpenWear Stereo Pro. Features differ by model, so buyers should check regional specifications for active noise cancellation, codecs, battery life, microphone performance and water resistance.

Bluetooth speakers for portable listening

Xiaomi’s catalog also includes portable Bluetooth speakers, including Xiaomi Bluetooth Speaker, Xiaomi Bluetooth Speaker Mini, Xiaomi Sound Outdoor and Xiaomi Sound Pocket. Depending on the model, these products focus on portability, outdoor use, stereo pairing or compact everyday listening. Confirm the exact model’s battery rating, charging connector, ingress-protection rating and regional availability before purchase.

How the ecosystem connects

Xiaomi’s “Human × Car × Home” concept aims to connect phones, smart-home devices, vehicles and personal audio. In compatible regions and products, the ecosystem can make it easier to move between devices and services. Features depend on model, software version, account region and local availability; compatibility should be checked before assuming a particular feature will work.

Availability note

Xiaomi’s vehicle specifications and prices are market-specific. Xiaomi Auto has announced a planned European arrival in 2027, while the official global site notes that the SU7 and SU7 Ultra are not currently for sale outside China. Audio product names and specifications can also vary by country.
