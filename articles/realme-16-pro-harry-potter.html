realme 16 Pro 5G Harry Potter Edition: Hogwarts Comes to Your Phone

realme has created a limited Harry Potter-themed version of the realme 16 Pro 5G, combining Hogwarts-inspired hardware details with customized software, accessories and a collector-style gift box.

A phone inspired by Hogwarts

The special edition gives the realme 16 Pro 5G a distinctly Hogwarts-focused identity. realme says the design combines the four Hogwarts house crests with an owl-inspired camera decoration, a textured back and a laser-engraved “Welcome To Hogwarts” detail.

A color-changing Hogwarts crest

One of the edition's most unusual design features is its light-sensitive back. According to realme, the Hogwarts four-house crest can change under UV light, revealing the colors associated with the four houses.

View realme’s official photos of the phone, Hogwarts trunk gift box, acceptance letter, house pins, bookmarks and themed earbuds ↗

Harry Potter software

The customization continues beyond the exterior. The phone includes Hogwarts Castle wallpapers, a Harry Potter-inspired boot animation, a themed fingerprint animation and more than 30 redesigned native app icons.

Camera inspired by Hedwig

realme gives the camera decoration a Hedwig-inspired design. The company lists a 200MP main camera with optical image stabilization, a 50MP selfie camera and a 112-degree wide camera in its published specifications.

Display and performance

The phone has a 144Hz AMOLED display, with realme listing up to 6500 nits peak brightness, HDR10+ support and a 3000Hz instantaneous touch response. The device uses the MediaTek Dimensity 7300-Max 5G platform.

Battery and charging

realme lists a 6500mAh battery and 45W fast charging for the Harry Potter Edition. The phone also supports bypass charging, a feature intended to reduce heat during certain charging and gaming situations.

The collector's box

The limited-edition package is part of the experience. realme describes a Hogwarts Acceptance Gift Kit containing themed collectibles, including an acceptance letter, house bookmarks, house crest pins and character postcards.
